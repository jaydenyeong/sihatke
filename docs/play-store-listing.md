## Full description (≤4000 chars)

**Stay connected without the fuss.**

Sihaty is a simple, private app for elderly people who want to reassure their loved ones every day — without phone calls, without complicated technology, and without giving up their privacy.

**For the person checking in:**
Tap twice a day. That's it. One tap for how your body feels, one tap for how your mind feels. Your family gets a small green tick. They stop worrying. You go on with your day.

- 10-second check-in, every time
- Large buttons, simple language, no clutter
- Set your own reminder times (1–5 times per day)
- Add an optional short note if you want to share more
- "Need Help" button sends an immediate alert to your emergency contacts

**For the family and carers (receivers):**
See your loved one's status at a glance in the Circle tab — green means great, red means they need help. Get a push notification only when it matters: when they report needing help, miss a check-in, or show a worrying pattern over several days.

- Traffic-light status for everyone in your circle
- Missed check-in alerts (configurable per contact)
- Declining trend alerts (3+ difficult days in a week)
- No constant pinging — only the notifications that matter

**Privacy first:**
We collect only what you choose to share. No location tracking. No selling of data. No ads. Your check-in history is yours.
