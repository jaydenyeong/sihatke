Sihaty — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 May 2026

Sihaty ("we", "us", "our") is a daily health check-in app that lets individuals share how they feel with trusted family members or carers. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. What data we collect

  • Account information: your full name and email address, used to create and identify your account.
  • Check-in data: the physical and mental status you self-report (e.g. "Feeling Great", "Need Help"), the optional short note you add, and the time of each check-in.
  • Contact information you enter: names, phone numbers, email addresses, and relationship labels of the people you add as trusted contacts. We do not access your device's contacts.
  • Push notification token: a device token that allows us to send you reminders and alerts. We do not use this for any purpose other than delivering notifications from Sihaty.
  • Timezone: used to schedule reminders at the correct local time.

2. What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your location at any time.
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, or device contacts.
  • We do not collect any biometric data.
  • We do not serve ads or share data with advertisers.
  • We do not sell your data to any third party.

3. How we use your data

  • To show your check-in history to you.
  • To notify the trusted contacts you have chosen when you report needing help, miss a check-in, or show a declining trend — but only if you have enabled those notifications per contact.
  • To send you reminders at the times you have configured.
  • To authenticate your account securely.

4. Data storage and security

Your data is stored in a secure Postgres database hosted by Supabase (EU region). All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plain text. We use short-lived JWT tokens for session management, stored in your device's secure storage.

5. Data sharing

We share your check-in status only with the specific trusted contacts you have added and who are also Sihaty users — and only for the notification types you have enabled per contact. We do not share any data with third parties for advertising or analytics purposes.

We use the following sub-processors:

  • Supabase — database hosting (supabase.com)
  • Render — API server hosting (render.com)
  • Expo — push notification delivery (expo.dev)

6. Your rights

  • Access: you can see all your data in the app at any time.
  • Correction: you can update your name, check-in schedule, and contacts in Settings.
  • Deletion: to delete your account and all associated data, email us at the address below. We will process your request within 30 days.
  • Portability: you may request a copy of your data in JSON format.

7. Children

Sihaty is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
